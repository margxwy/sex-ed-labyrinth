ABOUT PROJECT

Sex Ed. Labyrinth focuses on providing sexual education to young people 🫶 aged between 13-19 years old in Estonia, Albania, Moldova, Ukraine, and Lithuania. The project aims to help teenagers prevent major mistakes in their first sexual experiences, show them how to create their own constitution regarding what other people are allowed and not allowed to do to them, and help them understand that they have their own rights and perspectives.

The project takes the form of an engaging game that provides comprehensive sexual education in a fun and interactive way. The game covers a range of topics, including safe sex practices, consent, and healthy relationships, and is designed to be accessible and engaging for young people.

The primary objectives of the project are to empower young people to make informed choices about their sexual health and wellbeing, promote healthy attitudes towards sex and relationships, and help young people understand that they have their own rights and perspectives. Through this project, we hope to equip young people with the knowledge and tools they need to navigate their sexual experiences safely and confidently, and to help them build healthy and fulfilling relationships.

We believe that this project will play an important role in promoting sexual education and wellbeing among young people coming from inclusive backgrounds in Estonia, Albania, Moldova, Ukraine, and Lithuania.
